The score is the OMR score, or your MuseScore edit once you have reloaded one. Black: the audio agrees · OMR note the audio did not hear · +N flag: N notes heard near that note that the OMR score does not have (click it to hear and decide them) · g flag: a short note heard just before that note, maybe a grace note the OMR missed (a hint: about a third are real) · kept · dropped · sounding now.
