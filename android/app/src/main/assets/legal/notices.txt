Score from audio is built on other people's research, software and data. Thank you to all of them.

# Note detection: Transkun

The notes are found by Transkun, by Yujia Yan, Frank Cwitkowitz and Zhiyao Duan ("Skipping the Frame-Level: Event-Based Piano Transcription With Neural Semi-CRFs", NeurIPS 2021, and its follow-up work). Code and weights: https://github.com/Yujia-Yan/Skipping-The-Frame-Level, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2021 Yujia Yan.

The app runs the published Transkun 2.0 weights, converted to ONNX and partly quantised to 8-bit integers by us. Those weights were trained on the MAESTRO dataset.

MAESTRO v3.0.0: Curtis Hawthorne, Andriy Stasyuk, Adam Roberts, Ian Simon, Cheng-Zhi Anna Huang, Sander Dieleman, Erich Elsen, Jesse Engel and Douglas Eck, "Enabling Factorized Piano Music Modeling and Generation with the MAESTRO Dataset", ICLR 2019. Google LLC. Licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/

# Notation: our model, bd1

The notes are written as a score by a notation model we trained ourselves. Its design follows the work of Tim Beyer and Angela Dai ("End-to-End Piano Performance-MIDI to Score Conversion with Transformers", ISMIR 2024). The weights are ours and are licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/. Changes from the training data: performances were aligned to scores, encoded as note tokens, and used to train a transformer; the model contains no copy of the data.

It learned from:

- ASAP (Aligned Scores and Performances): Francesco Foscarin, Andrew McLeod, Philippe Rigaux, Florent Jacquemard and Masahiko Sakai, "ASAP: a dataset of aligned scores and performances for piano transcription", ISMIR 2020. Licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. https://github.com/fosfrancesco/asap-dataset
- PDMX: Phillip Long, Zachary Novack, Taylor Berg-Kirkpatrick and Julian McAuley, "PDMX: A Large-Scale Public Domain MusicXML Dataset for Symbolic Music Processing", 2024. Only scores marked public domain or CC0 were used, played back synthetically.
- A small set of jazz piano transcriptions we made ourselves.
